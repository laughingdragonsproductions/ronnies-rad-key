Why this site exists

Ronnie's Rad Key is one letter more than his name - on purpose. If Ronnie ever stumbles on this, I hope he sees how much his work shaped not just our playlist, but our whole creative direction.

Falling In Reverse didn't just soundtrack a phase of my life. The writing, the cinematic music videos, the attention to detail in every era - from chaotic early F.I.R to the full VFX RCU (Ronnie's Cinematic Universe) - pushed me to start more projects and branch out in ways I wouldn't have tried otherwise.

I love how deliberate the lore feels when you line the videos up: characters, callbacks, armor, sequels. That obsession with detail is why we built the Ronnie Cinematic Universe dataset behind this helix - and why we kept polishing the timeline until it felt worthy of the source material.

Working with Ronnie one day is a pipe dream, sure, but even if he just saw this and had a laugh, I'd feel this project was well worth it. Still, if this site ever reaches him, maybe he'll understand that a lot of what we're proud of in our own studio traces back to the vision he created.

Lit Hippies - our first music video

I've worn a lot of hats as a graphic designer and networking guy for years, but music and content creation was always the real goal. Ronnie's work is what finally shoved us off the sidelines and into an actual music video.

Laughing Dragons just posted Lit Hippies' Ghosts in the Greenroom - our first MV out the door. If you watch it with F.I.R.'s catalog in mind, you'll see how much of Ronnie's visual storytelling DNA is in there. We will never match the production bar Falling In Reverse has hit - and we're honest about that - but maybe one day life opens up enough that we can chase those heights for real.
